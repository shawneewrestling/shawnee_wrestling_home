Region Tournament Seeding Information (pasted by Jeff 2026-11-01)
1. Past advancements in previous state tournaments pre-seed the regions.
2. Committee: Regional Tournament Director or seeding chair (breaks ties) + 4 District Reps (one per district).
3. Each rep may bring one non-voting, non-speaking assistant. No other coaches.
4. One seeding meeting, no later than Tuesday of region week.
5. Purpose: a) ensure criteria followed; b) district champs get round-1 BYE; c) Pre-Seed, Head-to-Head movement, seed all 1st/2nd/3rd district placers and/or replacements.
6. All district champs must be seeded in top 8.
 a) A champ seeded outside top 8 is placed at 8 (multiple: 7 and 8, etc.), others shift.
 b) Lowest four 2nd/3rd seeds paired with highest four 2nd/3rd: best 2nd/3rd vs lowest 2nd/3rd, 2nd best vs 2nd lowest, etc.
7. A medical forfeit during districts counts as a loss for seeding purposes only.
Head-to-head cycle rule (Jeff): any loop of results of 3+ wrestlers eliminates all bouts in the loop from H2H.

--- Region Pre-Seeding Criteria (Jeff 2026-11-01) ---
1. Own merit in previous tournament, this level or better: higher placement/further WB advancement; then more appearances, most recent preferred. All get credit for being here (minimum advancement).
2. Defeated a seeded wrestler this season in this weight (better pre-seed = better win; multiple distinct opponents > same opponent twice).
3. Defeated any prior-year tournament participant this season (better advancement = higher; distinct opponents preferred).
4. Tie break: total Trackwrestling points, not win %.

--- Region Final Seeding ---
1. Head-to-head this season.
 a. First eliminate round robins of 3+ (A>B>C>A, A>B>C>D>A). A single loss may be used for as many round robins as needed.
   i. Multiple losses to the same opponent: eliminate only one of them.
   ii. Multiple results between two wrestlers within a round robin: eliminate the earliest result.
 b. Start with the best pre-seed and work down; best challenge heard first; vote only for extenuating circumstances. Majority of victories wins; a split goes to the later win.
 c. If TW points are tied, the district rep and seeding chair vote.
2. Unfair seeds: director emails the NJSIAA Tournament Director by noon Thursday; the state committee's ruling is final.
3. Re-seed on any replacement: the district's remaining 3 become 1st/2nd/3rd by best remaining; always reseed.
4. A replacement must be a district 1st-4th placer who competed in finals or consolations (unless injured); the 4th place finisher's coach is notified.
5. Forfeiture after weigh-in = eliminated (per NFHS).
