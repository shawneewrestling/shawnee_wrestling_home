State Individual Championships Seeding (pasted by Jeff 2026-11-01)
Committee: Wrestling Coordinator (breaks ties) + 8 NJWCA Region Presidents; meets at NJSIAA office, Robbinsville. Region presidents pre-seed assigned weights.
Seeds region 1st-4th place medalists (32 per weight). A region medical forfeit counts as a loss for seeding only.
Pre-seed: 1) own merit in previous tournament at this level or better (placement/WB depth; then appearances, most recent preferred; all get minimum credit); 2) defeated seeded wrestler(s) this season in this weight (better advancement = better; distinct opponents > same twice); 3) defeated prior-year participant(s); 4) tiebreak = total TW points, not win %.
Final: head-to-head identical to region (round-robin elimination 3+, one loss per repeated pair, earliest result in A>B>A>B series; work down from best pre-seed; majority of wins, split goes to later win). A vote moves the challenger ahead of the higher pre-seed (others drop) OR leaves the pre-seed order.
Replacement: region 1st-6th placer who competed in finals/consolations (unless injured); 5th/6th becomes the 4th rep.
Re-seed: appeals by noon Wednesday; wrestler out before 8pm Wed = reseed; region treats its remaining 4 as 1-4. No reseed after AC weigh-in; a legal replacement takes the spot; otherwise wrestlers move up and the forfeit goes to the highest seed.
